# MYKO — What Decay Becomes

## Logline
Myko touches a mushroom, follows its mycelium into a hidden cycle of decomposition, nourishment and renewal, and returns transformed into the mushroom-covered companion. Procedural Canvas animation with synthesized score.

## Numbers
1920 × 1080, 24 fps; drawings held on twos (12 fps); 120 BPM, 0.5 s/beat, 2 s/bar, 36 s, 18 bars, 864 frames, 14 shots.

## Structure
Act I 0–10: encounter/contact/inhabitation/eye entry. Act II 10–28: falling matter/decomposition/nourishment/fruiting/feeding/spores. Act III 28–36: eye return/physical transformation/quiet resolution. Changes in camera scale are real transforms, not image swaps.

## Shared geometry
G1: all microscopic entry/exit circles centre at (960,540), 240 px radius. G2: final Myko pose anchored at (880,835), feet on y=895; scene 12 ends in that pose, 13 and 14 use same pose and draw helper. G3: original mushroom warm apricot cap on log; contact finger touches crown centre, pulse begins there. Character owner defines exact local geometry in myko.js and reuses it across all seven shots. G4: forest owner keeps exact decaying-log and cap profile geometry across scenes 05/06/09/10 through nature.js.
