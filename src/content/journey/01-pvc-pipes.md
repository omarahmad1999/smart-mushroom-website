Unfortunately, I couldn’t find a Martha tent locally in Egypt, so I had to build most of the setup myself. I began by looking for a material that would be easy to assemble and resistant to moisture. PVC pipes seemed like the best choice.

First, I designed the frame using [Crafty Amigo](https://www.craftyamigo.com/pvc-pipe-builder-software) and calculated the number and lengths of PVC pipes I would need. That process led to the first version of the structure.

I then bought the pipes, cut them one piece at a time, and washed every piece in the bathtub before assembly. Once the frame was standing, it felt a little too wobbly because some of my cuts were not perfectly even. To make it stronger and more rigid, I added an X-shaped diagonal support.
