To enclose the tent, I needed to cover the PVC frame with transparent plastic. At first, I considered using UV-treated greenhouse plastic and contacted several factories to find it. However, they only sold it in large rolls of around 50 metres, which was far more than I needed. Since the tent would remain indoors and would not be exposed to direct sunlight, I decided that the additional UV protection was unnecessary and settled on ordinary transparent plastic instead.

I then measured the frame to calculate how much plastic I would need, covered the entire structure, and sealed the seams using moisture-resistant tape.
