I bought the Raspberry Pi 5 with 16 GB of RAM. This was more than the system strictly required, but I preferred to leave myself extra headroom rather than risk being limited later. It also gives me the flexibility to reuse the Pi for more complex projects in the future.

For computer-vision inference, I added a Raspberry Pi AI HAT+ 2. Its Hailo-10H accelerator allows the vision models to run locally without relying entirely on the Raspberry Pi’s CPU.

Because microSD cards are not ideal for sustained, write-heavy workloads, I decided to use the SSD from my old laptop as the system’s primary storage. I placed it inside a UGREEN SSD enclosure and connected it to the Raspberry Pi.

I couldn’t use a standard Raspberry Pi case because it did not have enough space for the additional HAT. Instead, I chose a KSSB-style case with enough clearance to accommodate both the Raspberry Pi and the accelerator.
