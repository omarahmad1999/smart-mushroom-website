I use two types of environmental sensor. The **SHT31** measures temperature and relative humidity, while the **SCD41** measures carbon-dioxide concentration.

There are four sensors in total: two of each type. One SHT31 and one SCD41 monitor the controlled environment inside the tent, while the second pair monitors the surrounding room. This allows me to compare the conditions created inside the habitat with the ambient environment outside it.
