Separate MOSFET modules serve the fan and LED. Device leads connect on the left, supply leads on the right, and the thin control wires connect to the T-cobbler.

### In the margin

Matching sensor-wire colours meet at Wago connectors before continuing to the breakout. The model simplifies routing; it is not a pin-assignment guide.
