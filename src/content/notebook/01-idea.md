A tent, some straw, and a way to pay attention. This experiment brings oyster mushroom cultivation together with environmental sensing, partial automation, and computer vision.

### Three questions to carry forward

1. How do conditions inside the tent compare with the surrounding room?
2. How do controller decisions relate to the environmental record?
3. What can repeated photographs tell us about visible change?

The room is an **environmental reference**, not a separate biological control crop. That distinction matters when interpreting the results.

*First incubation phase began 28 July 2026.*
