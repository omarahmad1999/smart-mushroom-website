I have always been fond of mushrooms since the day I learnt about them. I was fascinated by how distinct they are from the plant kingdom: how they consume the substrate provided to them, and how diverse and colourful they are. After a couple of at-home experiments to grow my own mushrooms, I thought: how about I create an automated system that gives my mushrooms the required habitat to grow efficiently?

### Three questions to carry forward

1. How do conditions inside the tent compare with the surrounding room?
2. How do controlled conditions increase the yield of the mushrooms?
3. How do controlled conditions grow healthier mushrooms?
