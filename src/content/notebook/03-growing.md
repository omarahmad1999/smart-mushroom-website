**Incubation → pinning → fruiting → harvest.** These are conceptual stages of cultivation, not a claim that this run reached every stage.

During incubation, mycelium spreads through the substrate. Later, small mushroom initials may appear before developing into recognizable clusters.

### Keep two records side by side

Photographs preserve visible changes. Grafana holds the environmental measurements and history. Neither record can explain everything on its own.

The documented incubation schedule calls for a full-resolution still every six hours from each camera. Selected captures from **19 August, 27 August, and 4 September 2026** are published on the site.
