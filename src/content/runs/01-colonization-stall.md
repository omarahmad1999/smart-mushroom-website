#### What I tried

Pasteurized straw, inoculated and hung in the tent to incubate, with every bag sealed inside three layers of plastic.

#### What happened

Colonization stalled. The mycelium never spread far enough through the straw for the bags to move on to pinning.

#### Why

Two things worked against it: temperatures of about 29 °C on warm days, and triple bagging that left almost no fresh-air exchange.
