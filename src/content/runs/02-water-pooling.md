#### What I tried

With the bags colonized, I removed their tops at the pinning and fruiting stage to give the cameras a clearer view.

#### What happened

Most bags rotted. Only one bag fruited, captured in the two-day film.

#### Why

Water from the humidifier pooled in the open tops and soaked the straw.
