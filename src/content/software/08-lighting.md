### What it does

Controls lighting according to the active growth phase and camera schedule.

### How it works

During **incubation**, LEDs remain off except briefly when images are being captured. During **fruiting**, the LEDs remain on according to the configured fruiting setup.

### Output / Destination

Controls the LED lighting and records its activity.
