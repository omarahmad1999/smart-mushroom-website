### What it does

Creates a consistent visual history of mushroom growth.

### How it works

During incubation, images are captured approximately every **6 hours**. During fruiting, captures occur approximately every **1 hour**. Before each image, the exhaust fan runs briefly to remove excess humidifier mist that would otherwise make the camera image appear white.

### Output / Destination

Images are saved with timestamps/experiment metadata and can later be combined into **timelapses**.
