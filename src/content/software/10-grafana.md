### What it does

Provides a visual interface for monitoring and analysing the experiment.

### How it works

**Grafana** queries the historical data stored in TimescaleDB and presents it through dashboards and time-series graphs.

### Output / Destination

Displays trends such as temperature, humidity, CO₂ and system/device activity over time.
